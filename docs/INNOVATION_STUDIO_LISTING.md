## Title

ReactCoach 11 — Adaptive AI Football Coaching

## Tagline

Know your strengths. Read your opponent. Make the better move.

## Description

ReactCoach 11 is an adaptive football decision-coaching platform that starts with two players: the player being assessed and their opponent. Using synthetic player profiles, tactical situations, and a deterministic Rust decision engine, the platform compares the assessed player's strengths and weaknesses with the opponent's likely resistance and vulnerabilities. It recommends corrective decisions and explains the tradeoffs in an interactive Next.js interface.

The concept spans player-to-player matchups, penalty situations, free-kick strategies, and offside positioning. Its central question is: What should this player do differently against this specific opponent in this situation?

The current prototype provides synthetic attribute-based matchup comparisons, editable player ratings, a transparent heuristic decision-fit index, and a simplified offside-position example. We are developing dynamic opponent reactions, stronger spatial simulation, Microsoft Foundry multi-agent coaching explanations, and an Azure-hosted demonstration.

To preserve credibility, player ratings and scenarios are fictional; fit indices are not calibrated success probabilities; and the offside demonstration checks relative position rather than making a complete officiating decision.

**Technology:** Rust, Axum, Tokio, Next.js, TypeScript, Server-Sent Events; planned Microsoft Foundry and Microsoft Azure.
